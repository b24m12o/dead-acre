    Challenges

    Challenges count across every run, even ones where you die. Most come in tiers, I to III: finish one and you get its scrap straight away, and the next tier opens. Some also unlock something new: a perk you can then buy before a run (Your kit), a starting loadout to take into night 1, or a whole new district to play.
